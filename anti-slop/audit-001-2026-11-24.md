# Anti-Slop Audit 001

## Design Read

Public tourism catalog for visitors and school coordinators, in the Nature Premium direction. Dials: ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1. Forest green anchors the identity, beige keeps reading surfaces warm, and gold marks primary actions; the page moves from introduction to the two catalog types with hover and focus feedback only.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH - Detail-page text contrast falls below WCAG AA (R-25)

In [show.blade.php](../resources/views/catalog/show.blade.php), `#64716B` is used for the price label and quota summary over the page's `#E3DAC9` background. The measured contrast is 3.68:1, below 4.5:1 for normal text.

### 2. HIGH - Dashboard empty-state label contrast falls below WCAG AA (R-25)

In [dashboard.blade.php](../resources/views/dashboard.blade.php), the `#A66D17` "Belum ada pesanan" label appears over `#F8F4EC`. The measured contrast is 3.97:1, below 4.5:1 for normal text.

### 3. MEDIUM - Account screens still use starter copy and title (R-20)

The login and registration pages retain English labels such as "Remember me", "Forgot your password?", "Log in", and "Register"; the browser title is "Laravel". These screens are part of the booking journey but do not match the Indonesian catalog and Nature Premium identity.

## Verification Context

- Pest: 37 tests passed, 144 assertions.
- Vite production build completed.
- The catalog migration was applied locally and all migrations report as applied.
- Browser checks covered the empty catalog, section links, skip link, mobile menu keyboard activation and Escape handling, and empty login/register submission. Viewports from 320px to 1440px had no horizontal overflow; section anchors resolved.
- Browser logs showed Vite connections and no reported page errors. Product and admin flows were exercised by feature tests; no sample products or admin accounts were added to the local database for browser testing.
